# Lightbox
Lightbox brings screenshot management and editing directly into Minecraft. Browse screenshots, save your favourites, and edit your images, all without leaving the game.

Lightbox is also fully client-side, meaning you can use it on your favourite servers!

<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/d8c18cd91df33931010a8952189ee3314fa012da.png" alt="An image showing the editor screen, editing a photo of a lobby on a minecraft server" width="75%"/>

## Gallery
### All of your screenshots in one place
- Browse your screenshots in a scrollable gallery
- Select and delete multiple screenshots at once
- Right-click to access a quick actions menu

<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/0f0bb98639d5d4ba016f2af99085f4f3fbcd12b5.png" alt="An image of the Gallery screen, showing the title 'Screenshots', a folder button, a 'bulk delete' button, and 8 screenshots with labels." width="75%"/>


## Viewer
### View your masterpieces in 4K HD*
- Pan and Zoom to focus on a specific point
- Rename your screenshots with a click of a button
- Favourite screenshots to keep them safe
- Delete any screenshots you don't need

<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/0fce89477b85b80ed3a19f006bdb05eadf00fdd3.png" alt="An image of the Viewer screen, showing an image of a Chicken Jockey at 2.4x zoom, along with buttons for renaming, favouriting, editing, and deleting. The screen also has the name, time, world, and coordinates of the screenshot." width="75%"/>

<sub>Does not come with 4K screenshots.</sub>

## Editor
### Bring out your inner Monet
- Pick from an RGB colour wheel
- Draw arrows, rectangles, and write text
- Crop the image to any ratio
- Save the image as a copy, or overwrite it

<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/cf907a8e6d74b4daeaac664ac40d72ce3883436f.png" alt="An image showing the editor screen, with an image of a pink sheep, with various edits. There are tools on the left, and a colour picker and size bar on the bottom." width="75%"/>


## Keybinds
### Speed things up
Editor shortcuts:
- Ctrl + Z: Undo
- Ctrl + Y or Ctrl + Shift + Z: Redo
- Ctrl + S: Save

Viewer:
- Left/Right or A/D: Cycle screenshots

In-game:
- K: Open Gallery
